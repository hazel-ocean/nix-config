## Local rules

These rules extend the upstream skill. Where they conflict, these rules win.

### Keep beads internal

- beads is a private tool. Do not reference it in anything another person reads.
- This covers Miro boards, Slack, Linear, GitHub PRs, issues and reviews, shared docs, email, and commit messages in shared repos.
- Do not include bead ids, `bd` commands, `project:` or `repo:` labels, or the word "beads".
- Restate the content in plain terms. Cite a link the team can open, such as the Linear issue, the PR, or the doc.
